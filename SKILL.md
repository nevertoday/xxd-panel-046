# XXD Panel 046 · 明亮厚涂微景观

Turn each supplied photograph into finished editorial artwork. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must rebuild the same source as a luminous, tactile impasto miniature carried by one source-derived diagonal field on bright textured paper.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve the source subject's identity through at least three specific cues while converting it into a tactile impasto miniature. It is not a trace, photo filter, generic oil-paint effect, clay model, plastic collectible, or unrelated landscape.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 046 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relationship → a recognisable tactile miniature with sculptural oil-paint volume → one clear diagonal support field derived from the source → bright source-derived colour and transparent-feeling light → a majority of warm-white textured breathing space → delicate art-publication typography that belongs to the same composition**.

The support field may become water, ground, path, light, or one abstract landscape slice only when that role follows the source. It must physically or spatially carry the miniature rather than float behind it as a stock paint swipe. Reflections, ripples, glints, extended strokes, clouds, sun, or mist are optional evidence-led accents, never a checklist.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the miniature, diagonal field, palette, light, or copy. Also reject dark brown oil filters, muddy grey, muted Morandi styling, fluorescent colour, candy colour, generic fantasy scenery, thick paint covering the whole canvas, toy packaging, polymer clay, glossy plastic CGI, e-commerce product rendering, or template poster decoration.

## 046 visual system

### Recognisable miniature subject

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Rebuild the subject as a refined micro-landscape entity with tangible scale and selective sculptural volume. Optimise viewpoint, proportion, and secondary detail only to make the miniature legible; never replace its identity.
- People keep recognisable pose and broad clothing shape; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth direction and mass; architecture keeps skyline, openings, and defining structure; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette, negative shape, and orientation; natural scenes keep one specific terrain, water, cloud, tree, or other source-bound anchor.
- Keep one principal subject or one genuinely inseparable relationship. Secondary scenery remains sparse and subordinate.

### Diagonal support and active whitespace

- Build one centred or slightly off-centre diagonal axis. It may be gentle or energetic according to the source, but never add a second competing axis.
- Let one translucent-looking yet materially impasto colour field support the subject. Its geometry, direction, and role must come from visible movement, terrain, light, water, path, or relationship in the photo.
- Preserve a majority of bright warm-white textured paper as active breathing space. The field and miniature should feel discovered on the page, not enlarged to fill it.
- Add only source-justified clouds, sun, mist, reflection, ripple, glint, or trailing paint. Decorative atmosphere without a semantic or spatial job is removed.

### Impasto material and luminous light

- Show real pigment build-up, palette-knife ridges, layered colour, broken raised edges, scraped transitions, paper tooth, and semi-hand-shaped irregularity.
- Concentrate thick paint on the miniature, support field, clouds, and selected highlights. Preserve thin or bare paper passages so the impasto has contrast.
- Make volume feel like oil paint with physical thickness and soft miniature depth, not photographic realism, smooth 3D modelling, resin, ceramic glaze, or clay.
- Keep illumination clear, warm, and breathable. Highlights may sparkle like sunlight but never become lens flare, neon glow, metallic chrome, or hard studio product lighting.

### Bright source-derived colour

- Extract the source's brightest, freshest, most life-bearing colours rather than averaging the entire photo.
- Raise lightness and clarity while keeping every major hue traceable to the source. Clear sky blue, lake cyan, tender green, warm yellow, coral orange, and peach pink are possible roles, not a fixed palette.
- Use a fresh warm/cool relation and abundant clean warm white. A small warm-orange, gold, or pink accent may act like sunlight when the source supports it.
- Keep colours vivid but not piercing. Avoid dirty grey, aged varnish, sepia, low-energy desaturation, cold tech colour, fluorescent pigment, and cheap candy palettes.

## Copy and typography belong to 046

046 text behaves like a refined annotation in an art publication—not a generic bold poster title, archival data wall, handwritten doodle, or UI label.

- Automatic copy derives one concise title from the subject identity, place when known, visible mood, or grounded symbol. It may be poetic and warm, but never invent a place, event, year, ownership, or backstory.
- Prefer one short title plus zero to two tiny supporting notes. Supporting notes are optional; never manufacture serial numbers, coordinates, or labels merely to fill the page.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within the user's permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: a generic “LIGHT / DREAM / JOURNEY / MEMORY” title that survives on another source is a failure.

Typography has its own 046 construction:

- **Role:** delicate art-publication annotation that calibrates scale and quiet space.
- **Voice:** luminous, precise, composed, and gently warm.
- **Construction:** light-to-regular editorial strokes, generous but controlled spacing, one compact title, and at most two much smaller notes. Do not impose the same fixed size ratio on every script or device.
- **Placement:** align with the diagonal support, subject edge, or one quiet white margin; preserve semantic phrase breaks and never scatter type decoratively.
- **Material:** lightly absorbed ink or dry letterpress on textured paper. Type remains crisp enough to read but must not look like a glossy digital overlay.
- **Script:** use a native editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Chinese and Japanese may use a refined Song/Ming or restrained humanist Gothic treatment; Korean uses natural contemporary Hangul with correct spacing; Arabic uses correctly connected, readable editorial Arabic with RTL composition; Latin scripts use an elegant light humanist sans or restrained contemporary serif according to the title. Never fake another writing system.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Composition commands

```bash

python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

# Design-only
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout design-only

# One wallpaper, repeated separately for all four devices
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --design phone.png --out wallpaper-phone.png \
  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```

`--size WIDTHxHEIGHT` overrides `--canvas` and source adaptation. Generate the design at the exact planned aspect; if exact pixels are unsupported, use a closest supported same-aspect bitmap and resample proportionally without cropping. Audit with the same layout used to build the result.
